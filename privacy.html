MiniJudge Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

MiniJudge lets you photograph your painted miniatures and receive a structured evaluation of your work. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Photos you submit or upload for evaluation and images you save to your Studio.
  • Account information such as your email address and sign-in method.
  • Onboarding answers used to personalize your experience.
  • Purchase entitlement confirmations; payment details are handled by Apple and our purchase provider.
  • Technical data such as device type, operating system version, and service logs used to operate the service and prevent abuse.

How we use information

We use information to evaluate photos, maintain saved miniatures, track progress, personalize the app, process subscriptions, keep the service secure, and comply with law. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Photo processing and service providers

Photos are transmitted securely to OpenAI for image analysis and processed to produce evaluations and annotations. Photos are not shared with other users or used for advertising. MiniJudge relies on Supabase for hosting, database, and authentication; RevenueCat for purchase processing; and OpenAI for image analysis. These providers process information as needed to deliver the service.

Storage, security, and retention

Information is stored with infrastructure providers using encryption in transit and at rest. We take reasonable measures to protect data, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Photos, evaluations, and Studio data are retained until you delete them. You can delete saved miniatures and permanently delete your account in My Studio.

Your choices and rights

You may access, correct, or delete personal information. Contact us to request a copy of your data; account deletion is available in My Studio. You may stop further collection by deleting the app.

Children’s privacy

MiniJudge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service evolves and will update its effective date when material changes are made.

For privacy questions, data requests, or account deletion, email info@summit-distributions.com.
