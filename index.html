MiniJudge Support

Help for miniature painters using MiniJudge to get thoughtful feedback on their work.

Contact us

Questions or trouble with the app? Email our support team. Including your app version, device model, and a short description helps us investigate.

Please do not email passwords, payment-card details, or private account credentials.

Email MiniJudge Support

Subscription and purchases

MiniJudge subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account. To view or cancel a subscription, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. To restore an eligible purchase, use Restore Purchases in MiniJudge.

Account and privacy requests

For help accessing your account or to request account and associated data deletion, contact us at info@summit-distributions.com. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing a request.

Scan troubleshooting

Use a clear, well-lit photo with the miniature in focus. If a scan fails, check your connection and try again later. If the problem continues, email support with the approximate time and any error message.
